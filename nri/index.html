Coming home from abroad?

Arrange a car or bike before you land. We look after families visiting Kanyakumari district from the Gulf, Singapore, Malaysia and further afield.

Coming back for a wedding, for the December holidays, or just to see family — the last thing you want after a night flight is to start hunting for a vehicle. Sort it out from where you are now, and it will be ready when you arrive.

Tell us your dates before you fly

Send us your arrival and return dates on WhatsApp. We will confirm what is free and hold a vehicle for you. Nothing is paid until you are here.

We meet you at the airport

Trivandrum International is the nearest airport to Nagercoil, about two hours away, and most flights from the Gulf and Singapore land there. We can have the car waiting when you clear customs, or send a driver to bring you home. Madurai and Tuticorin work too — tell us where you are landing.

Keep it as long as you need

Most people visiting family stay for weeks, not days. Our daily rate comes down for longer hires, so a fortnight or a month costs considerably less per day than a weekend. See monthly rental for how that works, and ask us for the long-stay rate when you enquire.

Drop it back when you leave

Return the vehicle at our place, at your house, or at the airport on your way out. Whichever is least trouble on the day you are flying.

Licence and documents

Worth checking before you travel rather than after you land:

  • An Indian driving licence works as it is, provided it has not expired.
  • A licence from another country needs an International Driving Permit alongside it — carry both.
  • Bring your passport, and your visa or OCI card, as photo identification.
  • Send us a photo of your licence on WhatsApp before you travel and we will confirm it is fine, rather than you finding out at the counter.

Booking a wedding from abroad

If the trip is for a wedding, tell us early. Muhurtham dates fill up months ahead, and families usually need more than one vehicle — a decorated car for the couple and others for relatives arriving from out of town. We can arrange the lot together so it is one conversation rather than five.
