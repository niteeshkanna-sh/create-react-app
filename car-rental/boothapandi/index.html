Self drive car & bike rental in Boothapandi

Boothapandi is a quick delivery from Nagercoil and sits on the road heading north-east, which is the way out of the district towards Tirunelveli and Madurai. A fair share of the hires here are for exactly that — a run north and back over two or three days.

Getting a vehicle in Boothapandi

Boothapandi is about 11 km from our base in Nagercoil, and we deliver rather than asking you to come and collect. Give us the address and a time; the vehicle arrives with its papers, a full tank noted down and the KM reading written in front of you.

The road north climbs gradually and the wind through the gap is strong enough to be felt in a small car, particularly in the afternoon. It is a well surfaced road and an easy drive; the wind is simply worth knowing about before you meet it.

Around Boothapandi

Known for the town on the Pazhayar, on the road north towards Tirunelveli.
